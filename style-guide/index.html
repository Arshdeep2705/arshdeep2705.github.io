Contrast pairs

Ratios are calculated from the palette when the site is built. Body text needs 4.5 to 1 for Level AA and 7 to 1 for Level AAA.

  • Ink on Linen 14.13 to 1Body text on the page
  • Ink on Paper 14.99 to 1Body text on a raised surface
  • Stone on Linen 8.17 to 1Muted text on the page
  • Evergreen on Linen 7.31 to 1Link on the page
  • Evergreen on Paper 7.76 to 1Link on a raised surface
  • White on Evergreen 7.88 to 1Primary button label, footer text
  • Ink on Ember Soft 10.34 to 1Text on a soft panel
  • Error on Paper 10.05 to 1Error text
  • Linen on Night 15.89 to 1Dark theme body text
  • Stone Light on Night 11.05 to 1Dark theme muted text
  • Evergreen Light on Night 10.26 to 1Dark theme link

Spacing

Spacing is set in rem, so it grows with the reader's text size. Sections use a fluid pad from 3.5rem to 7rem. The page gutter is 1.25rem. The content width is 76rem, or 46rem for reading pages.
